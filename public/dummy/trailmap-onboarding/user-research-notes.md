Onboarding usability notes (Trailmap)

Moderated sessions, 7 participants who'd never used the app before,
recruited across a mix of self-reported fitness levels. Task: "sign up and
find a route you'd actually consider doing this weekend."

- 5 of 7 scrolled past at least the first 10 routes before saying anything
  resembling "I don't know which of these are for me."
- One participant (self-described beginner) opened a route rated for
  experienced hikers, read the description, and said "I have no idea if
  this is something I could do" before giving up and closing the app.
- A more experienced participant had the opposite complaint — too much
  scrolling through easy routes to find anything that felt worth their
  time.
- Nobody complained about the route cards themselves (photo, distance,
  elevation were all called out as useful) — the complaint was entirely
  about not knowing where to start looking.
- 2 of 7 didn't make it to saving a route at all within the 5-minute task
  window; both specifically cited not knowing which list to trust.

Quick note from a follow-up question ("would you be willing to answer 1-2
quick questions about your experience level before seeing routes?"): all 7
said yes, several said "honestly I assumed it would do that already."
