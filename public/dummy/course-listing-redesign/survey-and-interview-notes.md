# Research notes — course listing redesign (empathize phase)

Context: PW Learning App has 44.6 lakh+ paid users (FY25), and the course
listing page is the single biggest revenue surface in the app. The existing
batch card was cluttered and outdated — kicked off research to find out why
students were bouncing before enrolling.

## Survey findings (students on the course listing page)

- 30% of students said they couldn't find the batch they were looking for.
- 25% weren't familiar with our batch naming system — this comes up
  constantly on support calls too.
- ~45% weren't aware there were different batch types / SKUs at all — free
  batches, the "power batch", test series, books, etc. They just saw "a
  course" and didn't realize there were tiers.

## How do students actually pick a course? (by exam category)

Talked to students prepping for three different exam categories and asked
them to walk through their decision process out loud.

- **UPSC**: exam level, optional subject coverage & star teachers.
- **JEE/NEET**: star teachers, language, start date & learning mode.
- **CA**: level & attempt, star teachers & add-ons like handwritten notes.

Teachers come up in literally every category — that's the one constant
across all three. Everything else (language, dates, SKUs) is secondary and
exam-specific, so it shouldn't compete with teacher visibility on the card.
