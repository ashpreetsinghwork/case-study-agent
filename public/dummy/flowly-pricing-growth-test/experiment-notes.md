Growth experiment notes — pricing page social proof (Flowly)

Hypothesis: visitors landing on the pricing page have no signal that
other teams actually trust the product — it's just plans and features.
Adding lightweight social proof (customer count + a couple of short
testimonials) near the plan cards should reduce hesitation and lift
signup conversion, without changing pricing or copy itself.

What we shipped for the test:
- A single line under the page header: "Trusted by 12,000+ teams."
- A 3-card testimonial strip directly above the plan comparison table,
  pulled from existing G2 reviews (reused verbatim, just reformatted).
- Nothing else on the page changed — same plans, same prices, same CTA
  copy — to keep this a clean single-variable test.

Why keep it this small: previous attempts at bigger pricing-page
overhauls took 6+ weeks and made it hard to tell which change actually
moved the needle. This was scoped to ship in under a week specifically so
we could test ONE lever cleanly.

Qualitative notes from the post-test survey (sent to a sample of
converted signups):
- A few people specifically mentioned "seeing other companies use it"
  as a reason they trusted the product enough to start a trial.
- No one mentioned the testimonials negatively or found them
  distracting — low risk even if the lift hadn't shown up.
