# Reorder button — pilot metrics

Shipped to 50% of returning users (3+ orders in the last 30 days) as an
A/B test, ran for 3 weeks.

| Metric | Control (old flow) | With reorder button |
|---|---|---|
| Time from app-open to checkout (repeat order) | 2m 10s | 38s |
| Repeat-order completion rate | 61% | 79% |
| Orders per returning user / week | 1.4 | 1.6 |

Support tickets mentioning "wrong item" or "had to redo my order" dropped
noticeably in the test group too, though we didn't have clean tagging on
that metric going into the pilot, so treat it as directional, not a
confirmed number.

Team: 1 product designer (me), 1 engineer, part-time PM support.
Shipped in under 4 weeks start to finish.
