# Project facts, numbers & user reviews

## Project facts

- Timeline: April 2025 - ongoing (still live and growing)
- Company: PW (Physicswallah)
- My role: branding, product design

## The numbers (as of this write-up)

- 5.57L paid subscribers to date
- 50K+ daily active users
- 30Cr revenue so far

## What students are actually saying

Pulled straight from app store / in-app reviews:

- "Yeh PW ka netflix hai 🔥" (translation: "this is PW's Netflix")
- "Best feature in PW App"
- "Sir doubts wala pen feature best hai, sab clear samajh me aaya"
  (translation: "the doubt-solving pen feature is the best, everything
  became clear")

## Takeaways

1. Force-fitting AI doesn't work unless you integrate it within proper
   workflows.
2. Autopay is tough, but not impossible — with the right words and
   transparency, it doesn't sound scary.
3. We say bottom sheets are less intrusive than full screens, but they
   are also equally annoying.
