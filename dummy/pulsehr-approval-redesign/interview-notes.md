Interview notes — leave approval friction (PulseHR)

8 follow-ups off the back of the survey (5 managers, 3 employees), 20-30
min each over video call.

Managers:
- "I genuinely forget these exist until someone pings me asking why
  they're still waiting. It's not that I'm ignoring it, it's just one more
  unread thing in an inbox full of unread things."
- One manager admitted she'd started just approving everything on sight
  without really reading the request, specifically to avoid the backlog
  building up — a workaround, not a fix.
- Two managers separately asked for the exact same thing unprompted: "can
  this just show up somewhere I already check every day."

Employees:
- "I submitted it and then just... didn't know what happened. Is it stuck?
  Did they see it? I ended up messaging my manager directly to ask, which
  felt awkward."
- A recurring complaint: the status page says "Pending" with nothing else
  — no name, no timestamp, no sense of whether it's been a day or two
  weeks.
- Nobody asked for a fancier request form. The actual form itself (dates,
  reason, submit) wasn't flagged as a problem by anyone.

Working theory going into design: this isn't a form-design problem, it's a
visibility problem on both sides — managers don't see the queue, employees
don't see the status. Scoping to just those two screens, not touching the
request form itself.
