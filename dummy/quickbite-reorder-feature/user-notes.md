Quick notes — repeat-order friction (QuickBite app)

Looked at session recordings + did 6 quick intercept interviews with
users who order 3+ times a week.

- Almost everyone orders the "usual" at least half the time — same
  restaurant, same items. But there's no shortcut for that; they re-browse
  the whole menu every single time.
- One user: "I basically know what I want before I even open the app, I
  just have to click through five screens to get there."
- Two users said they'd abandoned a reorder entirely because an item they
  wanted was slightly different (e.g. "no onions") and re-customizing from
  scratch felt like too much friction on a phone.
- Order history page exists but is buried three taps deep — most people
  didn't know it was there until we pointed it out.
- Nobody asked for anything fancy — the ask was consistently "just let me
  order that again."

Proposed fix: a "Reorder" button surfaced directly on the home screen for
the last 3 distinct orders, one tap to add the exact same items (with
customizations) straight to cart. Skip the menu entirely.

Scope check: this is NOT a menu redesign or a loyalty program — just the
one button and the reorder flow behind it. Kept deliberately small so we
could ship and measure within a month.
