# Positioning notes — new learning product (codename: Pi)

## Why we need something new

PW's existing live-course model has real constraints we keep running into:

- Fixed set of teachers per batch — students can't switch.
- Daily live classes — no flexibility for students with other commitments.
- Structured content — fixed curriculum, no browsing/exploring.
- Full year course purchased at once — high commitment, high price anxiety.
- Targets one exam — a student prepping broadly can't mix content.
- Fixed test & revision timelines — no self-paced option.

Question we kept coming back to: how do we position a new product
differently from live courses? Does it solve for *surplus* learning
(on top of a live course) or *complete* learning on its own?

## Competitive scan

Looked at three apps that are each closest to a piece of the vision:

- **Seekho** — short-form learning. Good for bite-sized content, bad for
  depth.
- **YouTube** — creator-based content. Good for teacher-led variety, bad
  for structure/curation.
- **Netflix** — personalization & recommendation. Good for "what do I
  watch next" discovery, nothing education-specific.

None of them is "it," but the answer sits in between: long-form content
with teachers as the hero — like an OTT platform, but for education.
Students should be able to learn from any PW teacher at their own pace,
and practice/attempt tests whenever they want, not on a fixed class
schedule.
