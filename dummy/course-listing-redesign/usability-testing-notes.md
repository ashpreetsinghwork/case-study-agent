# Usability session notes — card variant testing

9 students total, 3 per exam category (UPSC / JEE-NEET / CA), 1:1 over
Google Meet with screen-share on the prototype (all four card variants from
card-variant-feedback.md).

Primary task/question asked to every student:

> "You're here to enroll in a course of your choice, how would you normally
> go about it?"

Follow-up questions asked after they finished the task:

1. What did you think about the information organised on the batch card?
2. What information did you expect to see on the card and were you able to
   find it?
3. What is the most important information you're looking for?
4. Did you feel there is something that was unnecessary or missing?

## Key takeaways (synthesized after all 9 sessions)

- Teachers need to be clearly visible at a glance on the batch card.
- Students usually skim through the page — different batch types need a
  visual indicator, not just text buried in the name.
- Search needs better visibility on the page for students who already know
  what they want to purchase (they don't want to browse at all).

Variant 4 (goal & teacher-focussed) tested best on teacher visibility, which
matched what we expected from the survey, but it wasn't compact enough on
its own — final direction borrowed the teacher treatment from variant 4 and
the information density from variant 1. See final-card-iterations.png for
where that landed.
