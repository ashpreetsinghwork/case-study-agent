# Card variant exploration — design crit notes

Four directions explored for the new batch card before taking anything to
usability testing. Screenshots attached for each.

1. **Compact card, name-focussed** (`card-variant-1-name-focussed.png`) —
   compact, but doesn't highlight star teachers prominently. Teachers got
   buried under the batch name and dates.
2. **Compact card, teacher-focussed** (`card-variant-2-teacher-focussed.png`)
   — only shows one teacher at a time, which delays the student's decision
   since most batches have multiple teachers on them.
3. **Vertical card, teacher-focussed** (`card-variant-3-vertical-teacher.png`)
   — student has to scroll within the card to see all teachers. Felt like
   more effort than it should be for something this important.
4. **Vertical card, goal & teacher-focussed**
   (`card-variant-4-goal-and-teacher.png`) — not as compact as the others,
   but puts the spotlight on teachers, which lines up with what research
   said mattered most across all three exam categories.

Took all four into the usability round (see usability-testing-notes.md) to
see which one actually held up with real students instead of just going with
the one we liked most in crit.
