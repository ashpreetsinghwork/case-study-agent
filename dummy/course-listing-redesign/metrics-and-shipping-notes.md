## Business impact (post-launch)

- ARPU: +2.42% relative uplift
- Conversion (listing → enroll): +4.4% relative uplift
- Annual revenue impact: ~40Cr

Numbers above are relative upticks from the production rollout, not a
pilot — this shipped to all users on Android, iOS and Web.

## Side project that came out of this: thumbnail generation

Once we standardised thumbnails across most batch types, manually making one
took 30-40 minutes (design software, exporting, uploading). Too slow once we
had hundreds of batches needing refreshed thumbnails ahead of launch.
Vibecoded a small internal tool on Lovable that generates a thumbnail in
about 30 seconds instead of 30-40 minutes. Not polished, but it unblocked
the GTM team from being bottlenecked on design for every batch refresh.

## What this taught me

1. Students, even from tier 2/3 cities in India, use the most modern apps
   like YouTube & WhatsApp — don't underestimate their expectations just
   because of the market.
2. Displaying the most important information helps more than listing a lot
   of information.
3. First fold is the key. Whatever is listed there gets the clicks, no
   matter whether it's actually the important thing or not.
