# A/B test results — pricing page social proof

Ran for 2 weeks, 50/50 split on pricing-page traffic, roughly 8,400
visitors per arm.

| Metric | Control | With social proof |
|---|---|---|
| Pricing page → signup conversion | 4.8% | 6.1% |
| Signup → active trial (day 1) | 71% | 73% |

The signup conversion lift (4.8% → 6.1%, a 27% relative lift) was the
primary metric and hit statistical significance a few days before we
called the test. The trial-activation difference is smaller and we're
treating it as noise, not a real effect, given the sample size.

Team: 1 product designer (me) + 1 PM, reused an existing engineer sprint
slot. Full test, from spec to shipped results, took 2 weeks.

Rolled the variant out to 100% of pricing-page traffic after the test
concluded.
